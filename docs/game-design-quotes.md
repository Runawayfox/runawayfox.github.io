# 게임 디자인 명언 모음

## Raph Koster — 게임 이론가, A Theory of Fun 저자

1. **"Fun is just another word for learning."**
   - 출처: *A Theory of Fun for Game Design* (Paraglyph Press, 2004)

2. **"A good game is one that teaches everything it has to offer before you stop playing it."**
   - 출처: *A Theory of Fun for Game Design* (2004)

3. **"Boredom is the brain saying, 'You're wasting my time.'"**
   - 출처: *A Theory of Fun for Game Design* (2004)

4. **"With games, learning IS the drug."**
   - 출처: GDC Online 2012 기조연설 "A Theory of Fun, 10 Years Later"

## Jesse Schell — 카네기멜런대 교수, The Art of Game Design 저자

5. **"A game is a problem-solving activity, approached with a playful attitude."**
   - 출처: *The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses* (Morgan Kaufmann, 2008)

6. **"The game is not the experience. The game is the thing that creates the experience."**
   - 출처: *The Art of Game Design* (2008)

7. **"Stop thinking about your game and start thinking about your player."**
   - 출처: *The Art of Game Design* (2008), Lens #1: The Lens of Experience

## Hideo Kojima — Metal Gear Solid 시리즈 크리에이터

10. **"90 percent of what is considered 'impossible' is, in fact, possible. The other 10 percent will become possible with the passage of time and technology."**
    - 출처: GDC 2009 기조연설 "Solid Game Design: Making the Impossible Possible" (Joystiq/Engadget 실시간 보도)

11. **"It's important to me that my games aren't only 'fun'. I want them to carry a message or kernel of something that players can take and use."**
    - 출처: *Edge* magazine #297 인터뷰

## Will Wright — SimCity, The Sims 크리에이터

12. **"I like to get people to think about the world around them, and have these little toy worlds in which they can run experiments."**
    - 출처: Celia Pearce 인터뷰, *Game Studies* vol. 2 (2002)

## Shigeru Miyamoto — Nintendo R&D

13. **"I have at last come to the conclusion that the role of a game designer is to design a complete game system by first comprehending the technologies that will enable and realize that system."**
    - 출처: GDC 1999 기조연설 (Jennifer deWinter, *Shigeru Miyamoto*, Chapter 5에 전문 수록)

14. **"If you want to build a house, you work on the second floor first. Similarly, I try to spend more time making the second half of my game more fun."**
    - 출처: Eurogamer 인터뷰 (2010.03.31)

15. **"Of course I could make Halo. It's not like I haven't thought about first-person shooters."**
    - 출처: Engadget 인터뷰 (2007.05.07)

## Katie Salen & Eric Zimmerman — 게임 연구자

16. **"A game is a system in which players engage in an artificial conflict, defined by rules, that results in a quantifiable outcome."**
    - 출처: *Rules of Play: Game Design Fundamentals* (MIT Press, 2003), p. 70

17. **"Play is free movement within a more rigid structure."**
    - 출처: *Rules of Play* (2003)

---

## Bernard Suits — 철학자

18. **"Playing a game is the voluntary attempt to overcome unnecessary obstacles."**
    - 출처: *The Grasshopper: Games, Life and Utopia* (University of Toronto Press, 1978)

---

## Johan Huizinga — 역사가, 문화이론가

19. **"Play is a free activity standing quite consciously outside 'ordinary' life as being 'not serious', but at the same time absorbing the player intensely and utterly."**
    - 출처: *Homo Ludens: A Study of the Play-Element in Culture* (1938)

---

## Jane McGonigal — 게임 연구자

20. **"Game design isn't just a technological craft. It's a twenty-first-century way of thinking and leading."**
    - 출처: *Reality Is Broken: Why Games Make Us Better and How They Can Change the World* (Penguin Press, 2011)

21. **"Games make us happy because they are hard work that we choose for ourselves, and it turns out that almost nothing makes us happier than good, hard work."**
    - 출처: *Reality Is Broken* (2011)

---

## Jesper Juul — 게임 연구자

22. **"Video games are half-real: they consist of real rules with which players actually interact, and fictional worlds that the rules project."**
    - 출처: *Half-Real: Video Games between Real Rules and Fictional Worlds* (MIT Press, 2005)
